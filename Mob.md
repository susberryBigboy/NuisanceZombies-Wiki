# Mob Behaviors & Changes

This mod drastically overhauls hostiles, introducing specialized behaviors, unique combat tactics, and enhanced AI to challenge even veteran players.

---

## 🧟 Zombie, Husk & Zombie Villager
* **Sprinting:** They sprint towards their targets instead of slowly shuffling.
* **Building & Breaking:** They build up with blocks to reach you and break through solid walls to breach your defenses.
* **Unerring Detection:** Hiding will not save you—they will detect and track you down anywhere.
* **Combat & Support:** Highly proficient with Maces, and they blow Goat Horns to call in reinforcements.
* **Vandalism:** They seem to detest crops and torches, actively trampling farms and destroying light sources.
* **Special Ability:** Ever heard of a *Stun Slam*? Better stay on your toes!

---

## 🕷️ Spider & Cave Spider
* **Spawning:** Cave Spiders can now spawn naturally in various environments.
* **Evasive Movement:** They employ advanced pathfinding tactics, such as zigzagging toward you or circling around to attack from behind.

---

## 🧜 Drowned
* **Master Swimmers:** Exceptional agility in water, making underwater combat significantly more hazardous.

---

## 🏹 Skeleton, Stray, Bogged, Parched & Wither Skeleton
* **Kiting Expertise:** Skeletons maintain optimal distance, keeping just far enough so your sword cannot reach them.
* **Tactical Positioning:** Quickly reposition themselves into strategic line-of-sight angles for precision shooting.
* **Wither Skeletons:** Devastatingly dangerous when equipped with bows.

---

## 💣 Creeper
* **Tricky Tactics:** Capable of pulling feints and leaping toward you to initiate a detonation.
* **Statue AI:** If you look directly at them, they freeze; the moment you turn away, they close the distance in a blink.
* **Airborne Assault:** Some will even leap off Phantoms right above you!

---

## 🔥 Blaze
* **Enhanced Agility:** Extremely light on their feet—they can dodge incoming arrows!
* **Upgraded Attacks:** Watch out for their revamped, hazardous fireballs.
* **Ambushers:** Did they always spawn right there...?

---

## 👻 Ghast
* **Improved Spotting:** Vastly increased detection range and precision.
* **Deadly Artillery:** Fires enhanced fireballs similar to the upgraded Blaze attacks.

---

## 🦇 Phantom
* **Kidnapping:** They dive down to grab and carry players into the air.
* **Weight Mechanics:** They struggle with heavy loads and will drop you midway. Wearing heavy armor is highly recommended to make yourself harder to carry!
* **Favorite Treat:** They love Rotten Flesh. If they spot any dropped nearby, they will release you immediately to feast on it instead.
