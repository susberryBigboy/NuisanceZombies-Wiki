# Nuisance Zombies

Nuisance Zombies is a mod that enhances mobs to turn your Minecraft world into an extreme, hardcore survival challenge.  
Fully compatible with both Singleplayer and Multiplayer!

---

## 📖 Overview
Installing this mod significantly buffs hostiles and increases game difficulty, offering a thrilling and tough survival experience.

---

## 🛠️ Installation Guide

### 👤 Singleplayer
* Simply place the mod JAR file into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.

### 🌐 Multiplayer
* **Server-side**: Installing the mod on the server side is sufficient to run its functionality.
* **Client-side**: Installing it on the client side as well is optional, but recommended for additional visual and functional features.
    * **Client-side Benefits**:
        * Access the in-game Config/Settings menu just like in Singleplayer. *1
        * Homing Arrows will highlight their targets with a **colored glowing effect**.

> ⚠️ **Note *1**  
> Non-admin players will have restricted access to configuration settings when connected to a server.
